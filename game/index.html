Help & Guide

WDRA Channel 9 went off the air at 3:14 AM on October 27, 1987. Its crews left six tapes at each location. Pick a tape, find all six, then get back to where you started — under the exit sign.

It freezes while you look at it — but watching drains your signal. Look away and it moves. Each tape you take makes it faster.

Listen. Tapes hum softly when you are close. The static, the footsteps and your heartbeat get louder when it is near — even through walls.
